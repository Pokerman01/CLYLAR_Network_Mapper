{# Switch for "scan all ports 1-65535", with an (i) hover card and a live estimate line. Needs `prefix` ('ns' / 'rescan' / 'dd'). The row around it (#_ap_row) is hidden for Discovery (it looks at no ports) by updateScanAdvancedForType() in base.html; a Custom scan hides the whole Advanced panel (it names its own ports with -p). The checkbox keeps its name (all_ports) and id (_ap_input): the forms and scripts read `.checked`. The line under it (#_ap_note) is filled by refreshAllPortsNote() in base.html from the target and the scan type: the number of addresses, a rough time (marked as an estimate) and, above scanner.ALL_PORTS_MAX_ADDRESSES (64), the sentence that the system will not accept it. #}
